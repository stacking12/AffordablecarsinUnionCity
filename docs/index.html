We are the lender, not a middleman. Our in-house financing means we write the loan ourselves and set terms around your real paycheck, not a credit score printed on a report.

About our affordable cars

Affordable does not mean worn out. It means a car priced so the payment fits next to your rent, your groceries and your MetroCard. Frequently asked questions

Short answers to what people ask us most on the phone.

Do I need good credit to buy a car here?

No. We approve customers with bad credit, no credit, past bankruptcies and past repossessions. Because approvals are made in house, we look at your income, your job history and your down payment instead of relying on a credit bureau.

What do I need to bring with me?

A valid driver's license or state ID, proof of income such as recent pay stubs, proof of address like a utility bill, and your down payment. Bring your insurance information too if you already have a policy, since that speeds up the same day delivery.

How much is the down payment?

It depends on the vehicle and your situation, and it is always discussed before you commit to anything. Call 551-529-5045 with a rough budget in mind and we will tell you honestly what is possible that week.

Can I really drive away the same day?

In most cases yes. If you arrive with your documents and down payment, the approval, paperwork, plates and insurance binding can usually be completed in one visit to our Bergenline Avenue office.

Will paying you help my credit?

That is the goal. Our credit rebuilding program reports on-time payments, so a year of steady payments here can put you in a much stronger position the next time you finance a car or apply for anything else.
